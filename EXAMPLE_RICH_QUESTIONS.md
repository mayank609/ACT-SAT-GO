## Physics Example 3: Force and Motion

### Question
A 5 kg mass is pushed across a frictionless surface with a force of 20 N. What is the acceleration?

### Options

**A:**
Using **Newton's Second Law**: $F = ma$

$$a = \frac{F}{m} = \frac{20 \text{ N}}{5 \text{ kg}} = 4 \text{ m/s}^2$$

The acceleration is **4 m/s²** in the direction of the applied force.

**B:**
**Calculations**:
- Given: Force = 20 N, Mass = 5 kg
- Frictionless surface (no friction force)
- Apply: $\sum F = ma$
- Result: $a = \frac{20}{5} = 4 \text{ m/s}^2$

**C:**
Using kinetic energy: $KE = \frac{1}{2}mv^2$

If initial velocity = 0 and final velocity = 4 m/s:
- $a = 8 \text{ m/s}^2$ (incorrect application)

**D:**
The acceleration equals the force: $a = 20 \text{ m/s}^2$ (mistake: didn't divide by mass)

## Geometry Example 5: Triangle Properties

### Question
A right triangle has legs of length **3 cm** and **4 cm**. Which statement is correct?

### Options

**A:**
By the **Pythagorean Theorem**: $a^2 + b^2 = c^2$

$$3^2 + 4^2 = c^2$$
$$9 + 16 = c^2$$
$$c = \sqrt{25} = 5 \text{ cm}$$

The hypotenuse is **5 cm**. This is a 3-4-5 **Pythagorean triple**.

**B:**
**Right triangle calculations**:
- Legs: $a = 3$ cm, $b = 4$ cm
- Using Pythagorean theorem: $c = \sqrt{a^2 + b^2} = \sqrt{9+16} = 5$ cm
- Area: $A = \frac{1}{2}ab = \frac{1}{2}(3)(4) = 6 \text{ cm}^2$
- Perimeter: $P = 3 + 4 + 5 = 12$ cm

**C:**
The hypotenuse $c = \sqrt{3+4} = \sqrt{7} \approx 2.65$ cm (incorrect application)

**D:**
**Analyzing the triangle**:
- This is a **special right triangle** (3-4-5 triangle)
- Hypotenuse: $c = 7$ cm (arithmetic error)
- Angles include a 90° angle plus two acute angles

## English/SAT Example 6: Grammar

### Question
Choose the sentence that best corrects the grammar error.

### Options

**A:**
**Original**: "Each of the students have completed their assignment on time."

**Corrected**: "Each of the students **has** completed their assignment on time."

**Explanation**: "Each" is a **singular subject**, requiring a **singular verb** (has, not have). The phrase "of the students" is a prepositional phrase that doesn't affect subject-verb agreement.

**B:**
**Issue**: The original sentence incorrectly uses:
- Plural verb "have" with singular subject "Each"
- Violates **subject-verb agreement** rule
- Correction: Use singular verb "**has**"

**C:**
"Each of the students have completed their assignment on time" is **correct** (incorrect assessment)

**D:**
**Grammatical Analysis**:
- Subject: "Each" (singular)
- Verb: "have" (plural form - **WRONG**)
- Correct form: "has" (singular form)
- Rule: **Every singular subject requires a singular verb**

## Statistics Example 7: Data Analysis

### Question
A dataset has values: **2, 3, 5, 7, 8, 9, 12**. Calculate the **mean**, **median**, and **mode**.

### Options

**A:**
**Statistical Measures**:
$$\text{Mean} = \frac{\sum x}{n} = \frac{2+3+5+7+8+9+12}{7} = \frac{46}{7} \approx 6.57$$

$$\text{Median} = \text{middle value} = 7$$

$$\text{Mode} = \text{most frequent} = \text{No mode (all values appear once)}$$

**B:**
- **Mean**: Average of all values = 6.57
- **Median**: The middle value when sorted = **7**
- **Mode**: Most frequently occurring value = **None** (unimodal distribution with no repeats)

**C:**
| Statistic | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| **Mean** | 6.57 |
| **Median** | 6.5 |
| **Mode** | 7 |

**D:**
Mean = 7, Median = 7, Mode = 7 (all the same - **incorrect**)

## Advanced Example 8: Multi-concept Integration

### Question (Physical Chemistry)
What is the **pH** of a solution with **[H⁺] = 1 × 10⁻³ M**?

### Options

**A:**
Using the **pH formula**:
$$\text{pH} = -\log_{10}[\text{H}^+]$$
$$\text{pH} = -\log_{10}(1 \times 10^{-3})$$
$$\text{pH} = -(-3) = 3$$

The solution is **acidic** ($\text{pH} < 7$)

**B:**
**pH Calculation**:
- Hydrogen ion concentration: $[\text{H}^+] = 10^{-3}$ M
- Using logarithmic scale: $\text{pH} = 3$
- Interpretation: **Strongly acidic solution**
- At **25°C**: $\text{pOH} = 14 - 3 = 11$

**C:**
pH = 10 (using incorrect calculation of $10 - 3$)

**D:**
**Analysis**:
- Given: $[\text{H}^+] = 1 \times 10^{-3}$ M
- Formula: $\text{pH} = -\log([\text{H}^+])$
- Calculation: $\text{pH} = -\log(10^{-3}) = 3$
- Classification: **Acidic** (pH 0-7), Neutral (7), Basic (7-14)
- Color indicator: **Red** in universal indicator
